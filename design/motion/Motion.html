Motion with a job

Every demo below runs on click, because motion cannot be judged from a picture. Nothing here is decoration: each one exists to say what changed, or where something came from. One curve, nothing further than 10px, nothing longer than 420ms.

Why you have not noticed any

Counted in ui/studio.css: nineteen animation declarations. Thirteen are in the first run — the one-minute explainer before round 0. Two are in Your ad, one is round 0's ring.

Your week, Your money, What people typed, Where and when, Returns, Rivals and Results have none at all. The whole motion budget went into the screen a player sees once, and none into the twelve weeks they spend.

One

The week lands

The moment of the game. Today the numbers simply differ. Proposed: each figure counts to its new reading and the new bar grows from the axis — so you see what moved rather than being handed a new page. 420ms, once, on the week you just ran.

Two

A revision moves, and the tick stays

The claim the whole simulation rests on is that a week's numbers are unfinished — sales keep arriving, returns come back. Today that is a dashed tick and a sentence. Proposed: the earlier bar rises to its new height and leaves the tick where it was first reported. The motion is the lesson, which is the only reason to spend it.

Three

Arriving somewhere

Sections swap instantly today. Proposed: 10px and a fade, staggered 40ms per part — enough to say the page changed and in what order to read it, gone before you could describe it.

Four

The material, not a shadow

The precision half. A card today is a flat fill inside a 1px line. Proposed: a hairline along the top edge that fades at both ends, as though light fell on it — depth with no shadow, no gradient and no extra colour. It is the difference between a box and an object.

Rules

What would keep it quiet

  • One curve. cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1), the one the Studio already uses. No second easing.
  • One reason. Motion says what changed or where a thing came from. Nothing moves to be interesting.
  • 10px and 420ms are the ceilings. Most things get 300ms and a fade.
  • Once. Nothing loops. The only looping animation in the game today is round 0's ring, and it marks an unanswered step.
  • Every one has a prefers-reduced-motion answer, as the eight blocks in the stylesheet already do.
