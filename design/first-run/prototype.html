How it is paced

One beat at a time, and the player holds the clock

No autoplay and no timers — a timer freezes in the browser pane and records nothing. A played beat collapses to one quiet line and stays on screen, so a re-reader can scroll back and nothing is lost. Skip sits on every beat but the last; the last offers Watch that again, which is also how the Guide reopens it.

Beats 5 and 6 shared one board until 2026-09-19, when the owner split them: the delay and the return are the two hardest ideas in the run, and they were sharing the board where attention is thinnest.

Motion

The review's own rules, not new ones

Transform and opacity only, no library — slc-review section 13. A beat enters on opacity and an 8px rise, 240ms on cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1) (row 2). Going back reverses the direction on cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1) (row 3). The ladder reveals row by row at a 50ms stagger with its bars growing from zero — row 5's end-screen reveal, applied to the same idea.

No counting tickers anywhere. Every rupee figure appears at its true value, because a ticker shows in-between numbers the engine never reported. Under reduced motion every movement is dropped and the fades stay. Four motions in all, inside section 13's cap of five to seven for the whole app.
