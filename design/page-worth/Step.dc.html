Your page was graded 8 on its own words. The site work adds 2 from week 4, when it goes live — the money leaves the week you commit it, and the page is unchanged until then.

The shape is the lesson. A flat run, one jump, another flat run — that is what waiting three weeks for site work to land actually looks like, and a reader sees it before reading a word. The scale still runs from 0 to 10 and is labelled, so the jump is not made to look bigger than it is; it is legible because the eye reads a break in a line, not the area of a bar. Drawn as a true step, never a slope, because the grade does not drift between weeks — it sits still and then moves once.
