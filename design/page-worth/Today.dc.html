Your page was graded 8 on its own words. The site work adds 2 from week 4, when it goes live — the money leaves the week you commit it, and the page is unchanged until then.

The step is there and nobody sees it. Weeks 1–3 are 77px, weeks 4–12 are 96px. A 19px difference inside a 96px bar, twelve times over, reads as one flat row. The chart exists to teach that the money leaves now and buys nothing for three weeks, and at a glance it teaches nothing.
